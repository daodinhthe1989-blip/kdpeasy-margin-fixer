What about the inside (spine) margin?

Books also need extra space near the spine so artwork isn't swallowed by the binding — KDP asks for 0.375" on books up to 150 pages, more on thicker ones. Your PDF tool handles that when it lays out the pages. If your book is over 150 pages, pick a 0.5" safe margin here to give yourself extra room.
